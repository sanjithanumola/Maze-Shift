How to Play
★
Goal & Extraction:

Guide your cyan avatar from the start point to the emerald extraction vortex at the opposite corner of the labyrinth.

🔑
Keys & Locked Doors:

Collect color-coded keys (Cyan, Amber, Violet) to unlock corresponding reinforced blast doors blocking your path.

⚡
Teleporters & Switches:

Step onto matching portal pairs to instantly warp across the maze. Step on pressure switches to invert gate barriers.

⚠️
Traps & Patrol Drones:

Spike traps cycle through Warning and Lethal phases. Patrol drones traverse specific corridors. Avoid crossing while active!

💡
Hints & Pathfinding:

Press H to reveal the optimal BFS path to the exit. Uses incur a modest score deduction.
