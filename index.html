How to Play
★
Controls on Phone:

Use the on-screen circular D-pad (tap or hold to continuously walk) or swipe in any direction across the screen.

🌀
Goal & Extraction:

Guide your cyan player node from the starting coordinate to the emerald extraction vortex at the opposite corner.

🔑
Keys & Locked Doors:

Collect color-coded keys (Cyan, Amber, Violet) to unlock corresponding reinforced blast doors blocking your path.

⚡
Teleporters & Switches:

Step onto matching portal pairs to instantly warp across coordinates. Step on pressure switches to invert gate barriers.

⚠️
Traps & Warning Cycles:

Spike traps cycle through Warning and Lethal phases. Stepping on active traps incurs time and score penalties!
